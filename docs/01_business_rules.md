# Business Rulse

- Customer minimum age must be 18 for all kind of operations
- Customer will request staff to perform all operations except reading account balance
- any kind of activty to forzen account is not allowd accept reading account balance
- minimum balance of the account is 0
- any transaction updating the account balance in negative is fail
- any transaction with wrong info is failed
- if account is closed no transaction is allowed
- Transaction will be recorded as success or fail or pending.
